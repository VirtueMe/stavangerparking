# Data licence

The raw snapshots on this branch are copies of open datasets, stored unchanged, as returned by each source. Both are published under the [Norwegian licence for Open Government data (NLOD) 2.0](https://data.norge.no/nlod/en/2.0) ([norsk](https://data.norge.no/nlod/no/2.0)).

## Stavanger parkering (`bronze/parking/`)

The dataset [Stavanger parkering](https://opencom.no/dataset/stavanger-parkering), published by **Stavanger kommune**.

> Contains data under the Norwegian licence for Open Government data (NLOD) distributed by Stavanger kommune.
>
> Inneholder data under Norsk lisens for offentlige data (NLOD) tilgjengeliggjort av Stavanger kommune.

## Parkeringsregisteret (`bronze/parkeringsregisteret/`)

Stavanger Parkering's parking areas in the national parking register, [Parkeringsregisteret](https://data.norge.no/en/datasets/a0cf9785-601e-4c5b-b19c-74b639b5819a/parkeringsregisteret), published by **Statens vegvesen**.

> Contains data under the Norwegian licence for Open Government data (NLOD) distributed by Statens vegvesen.
>
> Inneholder data under Norsk lisens for offentlige data (NLOD) tilgjengeliggjort av Statens vegvesen.

The code that collects and processes the data is licensed separately under the MIT licence; see `LICENSE` on the `main` branch of [VirtueMe/stavangerparking](https://github.com/VirtueMe/stavangerparking).
