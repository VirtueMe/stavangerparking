# Data licence

The raw snapshots on this branch are copies of the open dataset [Stavanger parkering](https://opencom.no/dataset/stavanger-parkering), published by **Stavanger kommune**. They are stored unchanged, as returned by the source.

> Contains data under the Norwegian licence for Open Government data (NLOD) distributed by Stavanger kommune.
>
> Inneholder data under Norsk lisens for offentlige data (NLOD) tilgjengeliggjort av Stavanger kommune.

- Licence: [NLOD 2.0](https://data.norge.no/nlod/en/2.0) ([norsk](https://data.norge.no/nlod/no/2.0))
- Source: [opencom.no/dataset/stavanger-parkering](https://opencom.no/dataset/stavanger-parkering)

The code that collects and processes the data is licensed separately under the MIT licence; see `LICENSE` on the `main` branch of [VirtueMe/stavangerparking](https://github.com/VirtueMe/stavangerparking).
